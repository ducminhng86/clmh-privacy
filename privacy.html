Privacy Policy — clmh.info Personal Backup App

Last updated: September 25, 2026

1. Who uses this app

This application is used exclusively by the owner of the domain clmh.info for personal backups. It is not available to the public.

2. What Google data the app accesses

The app requests only the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With this scope it can see and manage only the files and folders it has created itself. It cannot read any other files in the owner's Google Drive.

3. How the data is used

The app uploads, lists, reads back and deletes the owner's own backup files. All backup content is encrypted on the owner's computer before upload, so the stored files are unreadable without the owner's private key.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party. No analytics, advertising or tracking is used.

5. Storage and retention

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive and are removed automatically by the owner's retention schedule. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Limited Use

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Contact

Questions can be sent to the user support email shown on the Google consent screen for this app.

Tóm tắt tiếng Việt

Ứng dụng chỉ dùng cho mục đích sao lưu cá nhân của chủ tên miền clmh.info. Chỉ xin quyền drive.file (chỉ thấy file do chính ứng dụng tạo ra). Dữ liệu được mã hoá trên máy trước khi tải lên, không chia sẻ cho bất kỳ bên thứ ba nào. Có thể thu hồi quyền truy cập bất cứ lúc nào tại trang quyền của tài khoản Google.
